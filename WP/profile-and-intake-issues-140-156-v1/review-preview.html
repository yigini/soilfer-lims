What if the pit code is unknown?

Testing continues. LIMS records “not recorded” rather than inventing a code. A site, plot or composite sample keeps its true source meaning.

Why does the namespace matter?

Two surveys may use the same local code. Their namespaces keep those references separate. A saved reference must remain stable after a project is renamed.

Does commercial intake need a soil pit?

Usually the laboratory can test without it. A particular study or service can require it, but the generic commercial form should not force irrelevant agronomic questions.
