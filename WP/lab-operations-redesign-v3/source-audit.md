## Research supporting the design

1. [USDA Soil Survey Manual, chapter 3, pp. 123–125](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-09/The-Soil-Survey-Manual.pdf): authoritative texture-class definitions. It supports the independent 50/35/15 → Loam check, the 12-class scheme and explicit boundary treatment. The classification scheme and fine-earth size limits must be recorded with a result. The proposed application grouping is a design decision based on those outputs.
2. [FAO internal quality control](https://www.fao.org/4/w7295e/w7295e0a.htm): QC operates at batch level with controls and duplicates; batch size and rules depend on procedure/equipment. Forty samples is the user's practical target, not a universal scientific limit. QC insertions consume run positions and must remain identifiable.
3. [FAO soil sample pretreatment](https://www.fao.org/global-soil-partnership/glosolan-old/soil-analysis/sops/volume-1-3/en/): representative aliquots, homogenization and sample preparation matter. This supports tracing prepared material; it does not justify forcing every method through the same oven/milling checklist. The linked GLOSOLAN preparation and pH PDFs could not be fetched in this review; their numerical procedures are not claimed as verified.
4. [USDA laboratory information manual](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/SSIR45.pdf): a fixed SOC-to-SOM factor is an approximation. Do not silently convert between measured organic carbon and measured/estimated organic matter.
5. [FAO Water Quality for Agriculture](https://www.fao.org/4/T0234e/T0234E01.htm): SAR uses solution concentrations with specified equivalent units. Do not substitute exchangeable soil cations or mix molar and charge-equivalent units.

These references support operational requirements. They do not certify all procedures in this platform, a universal texture tolerance, a universal pH duplicate limit, instrument capacity or local SOP settings.

## Verification limits

The pure probes verify current utility behavior. No production draft, completion, approval, QC override or report was created. Browser review used the existing manager session; other roles were traced through source, not impersonated. All write-path and cross-lab findings require isolated end-to-end acceptance tests. Full local and production catalogue inventories must be compared before migration. No blanket “all methods verified” or “zero defects” claim is made.
