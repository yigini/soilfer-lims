# Equipment & Inventory

SoilFER-LIMS includes modules for tracking laboratory equipment and consumable inventory — two often-overlooked but critical aspects of laboratory management.

---

## Equipment Module

### Why Track Equipment?

Laboratory accreditation standards (such as ISO/IEC 17025) require laboratories to maintain records of instruments, their calibration status, and maintenance history. Even if you're not pursuing accreditation, knowing when your pH meter was last calibrated or when your spectrometer needs servicing helps prevent unreliable results.

### What You Can Do

- **Register all instruments** — enter name, model, serial number, manufacturer, acquisition date
- **Assign locations** — track where each instrument is physically located in the lab
- **Set calibration schedules** — define how often each instrument needs calibration
- **Receive alerts** — the system notifies you when calibration is due or overdue
- **Log maintenance** — record all maintenance activities with dates, descriptions, and costs
- **Track eligibility** — mark instruments as "eligible" (OK to use) or "ineligible" (out of service, awaiting repair, or overdue for calibration)

> 💡 When an instrument is marked as ineligible, any results entered using that instrument can be flagged, helping maintain data integrity.

## Inventory Module

### Why Track Inventory?

Running out of a critical reagent in the middle of a large batch of samples is disruptive and costly. The inventory module helps you plan purchases and track usage.

### What You Can Do

- **Create an item catalog** — define all the reagents, chemicals, and consumables your lab uses
- **Categorize items** — organize by type (acids, bases, buffers, glassware, filter paper, etc.)
- **Track stock levels** — record additions (when you buy) and withdrawals (when you use)
- **Set minimum thresholds** — receive alerts when stock drops below a specified level
- **Track lot numbers** — required for traceability in quality-assured labs
- **Record suppliers** — keep vendor information for easy reordering
