# Clients & Viewers: Reading Results & Reports

## What This Is For

Check the progress of your submitted soil samples, read approved analytical results and quality flags, and download signed PDF Certificates of Analysis from **Reports**.

> *Screenshot placeholder — docs/book/src/img/screenshots/viewer-reports-and-results.png (screenshot coming once the training data is ready): The **Result Reports** and **Analytical Results Master** screens showing **View report**, **Print / Save as PDF**, and **CSV** / **Excel** export buttons.*

---

## Before You Start

- Sign in with your **Viewer** account (or open the public report verification link shared by the laboratory).

---

## Steps

### Part 1: Check Sample Progress in **Samples**

1. Click **Samples** in the left menu.
   *You should see the list of soil samples shared with your project or laboratory.*
2. Look at the **Status** badge on each sample row:
   - **Received**: the sample arrived at the laboratory and is waiting for preparation or testing.
   - **In Progress**: technicians are drying, grinding, or testing the sample.
   - **Ready for final check**: bench testing is finished and the laboratory manager is completing final review.
   - **Approved**: all results are approved and ready for reporting.

### Part 2: Read and Download Your Certificate of Analysis

1. Click **Reports** in the left menu.
   *You should see **Result Reports** listing every released report with its **Sample / Lab ID**, **Client**, **Project**, **Version**, and **Generated** date.*
2. Use the search box (**Search by name, phone, project, sample ID, or lab ID...**) to find a specific sample or project.
3. Click **View report** in the **Actions** column to open the Certificate of Analysis on screen.
4. Read the sections of the certificate:
   - **Preparation records**: shows the actual **Drying** and **Preparation** completed before testing.
   - Analytical results table: shows the approved **Reported value** (the final number printed on the report), standardized measurement unit, method name, and fertility rating badge (see [Controlled Units & Agronomic Interpretation](agronomic-interpretation.md)).
   - Quality summary: shows **QC within limits for all reported values.** or lists any **QC deviations:** that the laboratory manager reviewed and accepted.
5. Click **Print / Save as PDF** at the top of the report window to save or print the official PDF.

### Part 3: Export Project Results to a Spreadsheet

1. Click **Data Results** in the left menu to open **Analytical Results Master**.
2. Filter by **Wet Chemistry**, **Spectral Only**, or a specific project code (**Project Code...**).
3. Click **CSV** or **Excel** to download the table of approved results.

---

## What Happens Next

You can share the downloaded PDF with farmers, agronomists, or project partners. Anyone with the printed certificate can scan its QR code to verify that they are reading the latest official version.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- *If you see* **No reports found** *in* **Reports** *→* Check **Samples** to see if your samples are still **In Progress**. A report appears in **Result Reports** only after the manager clicks **Authorize & release report**.
- *If a public link or QR code says* **This report is under amendment and is temporarily unavailable.** *→* The laboratory is updating or re-checking a result on that sample. Wait for the laboratory to release the new version.
- *If a public link says* **This report has been superseded by an amended report. Ask the laboratory for the current report.** *→* Sign in and open **Reports** with the **Published** filter selected to download the newest **Version**.

---

## For the QA Person

- **Viewer** accounts have read-only access limited to their assigned laboratory or project and cannot edit samples, work items, or quality control records.
- Every report displays its **Version** and states **Replaces** when an amended revision replaces an earlier certificate.
