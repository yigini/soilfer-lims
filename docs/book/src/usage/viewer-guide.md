# Reading Results & Reports (Clients & Viewers)

## What This Is For

Find your soil samples, read approved analytical results, and download signed PDF Certificates of Analysis.

---

## Before You Start

- Sign in with your viewer or project coordinator account on the **Sign in to SoilFER LIMS** screen.

---

## Steps

1. Click **Samples** in the left menu to check whether your samples have arrived, are in preparation, or have completed analysis.
   *You should see a list of samples shared with your project or laboratory.*
2. Click **Reports** in the left menu to find issued Certificates of Analysis.
   *You should see each report with its report number, issue date, and status.*
3. Click a report row to preview the results and quality summary, or download the signed PDF Certificate of Analysis.
4. For details on measurement units and soil fertility classes, see [Controlled Units & Agronomic Interpretation](./agronomic-interpretation.md) and [Certificates of Analysis, PDF Reports & Amendments](./reporting-pdf.md).

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **If you do not see your project samples →** Ask your Laboratory Manager to check that your user account is linked to the right laboratory and project in **Laboratory Staff**.
- **If a public QR code link says a report was replaced →** The laboratory issued an updated revision of the report. Open **Reports** to download the newest revision.
