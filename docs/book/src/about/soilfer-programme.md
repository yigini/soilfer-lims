# The SoilFER Programme

## What Is SoilFER?

**SoilFER** (Soil Mapping for Resilient Agrifood Systems) is a programme by the **Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)** that aims to help countries build better agricultural systems based on soil data and science.

Launched in **2023**, SoilFER addresses some of the world's most pressing agricultural challenges:

- 🌾 **The global fertilizer crisis** — farmers in many countries lack access to affordable, appropriate fertilizers
- 🌡️ **Climate change** — changing weather patterns affect soil health and crop productivity
- 🏜️ **Soil degradation** — overuse, erosion, and poor management are reducing the fertility of agricultural lands worldwide
- 📊 **Lack of soil data** — many countries don't have comprehensive soil maps or laboratory networks to guide agricultural decisions

## The Vision

SoilFER's vision is simple but powerful: **give farmers the right information about their soil so they can make better decisions about what to grow and how to fertilize it.**

This means:
1. **Collecting soil samples** from agricultural areas across participating countries
2. **Analyzing those samples** in well-equipped, quality-assured laboratories
3. **Building digital soil maps** that show soil properties across the landscape
4. **Creating decision support tools** that recommend the right fertilizer for each farmer's specific soil and crop

## The Three Pillars of SoilFER

### 1. National Soil Information Systems (NSIS)
Robust systems for collecting, managing, and sharing soil data at the national level. SoilFER-LIMS is a key component of this pillar — it manages the laboratory data that feeds into the national soil database.

### 2. Decision Support Systems (DSS)
Digital tools that translate soil data into practical recommendations. For example: "Based on your soil's pH and nutrient levels, here is the optimal fertilizer application for maize."

### 3. Sustainable Soil Management (SSM)
Promotion of farming practices that maintain or improve soil health — such as cover cropping, composting, integrated nutrient management, and reduced tillage.

## How Does SoilFER-LIMS Fit In?

SoilFER-LIMS is the **data engine** of the programme's laboratory operations. Here's how it connects to the larger picture:

```
Field Collection (KoboToolbox)
        ↓ samples and GPS data
┌───────────────────────────────┐
│       SoilFER-LIMS            │  ← You are here
│  Sample tracking, analysis,   │
│  QC, results, spectral data   │
└───────────────────────────────┘
        ↓ validated results
National Soil Information System
        ↓ soil maps
Decision Support System
        ↓ recommendations
Farmers & Policymakers
```

When a field worker collects a soil sample and submits the information through KoboToolbox, that data flows into SoilFER-LIMS. The laboratory receives the sample, performs analyses, enters results, and goes through quality checks. Once approved, the data becomes part of the national soil dataset that powers maps and fertilizer recommendations.
