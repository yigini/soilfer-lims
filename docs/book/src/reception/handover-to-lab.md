# Hand Over Accepted Samples to the Lab

## What This Is For

Check your reception handoff queue at the end of a session, place accepted sample bags onto drying trays or storage shelves, and resolve any intake holds before preparation begins.

> *Screenshot placeholder — docs/book/src/img/screenshots/reception-handoff-queue.png (screenshot coming once the training data is ready): The **Laboratory Operational Handoff** and **Recent check-ins** views showing accepted samples ready to move to the drying room.*

---

## Before You Start

- You have finished accepting and labelling samples in **Sample Reception**.
- The physical labelled bags are arranged on your reception counter or trolley.

---

## Steps

1. Click **Sample Reception** in the left menu. Review the **Recent check-ins** and **Laboratory Operational Handoff** sections on the reception dashboard.
   *You should see every sample you accepted today with its **Permanent Lab ID** and status **Accepted**.*
2. Check each physical bag on your tray against the screen list. Make sure every bag has its printed barcode sticker attached.
3. If you need to record which shelf or drying rack the tray is moving to right now:
   - Open the sample in **Samples** and click the **Sample & request** tab.
   - In the **Physical Material & Custody** card, click **Record storage movement**.
   - Keep **Custody event** as **Moved** and choose the destination shelf under **Storage location** (**Choose a sample storage location**).
   - Enter a short note in **Reason** (such as moving accepted samples to the drying room) and click **Save**.
4. Carry the tray to the drying and preparation area and hand it to the preparation technician.

---

## What Happens Next

Preparation technicians see the handed-over samples in **My Work** and **Workbench** under **Air Drying** (**Waiting for drying**). Every storage movement you record appears in the sample's **Custody events** list on the **Sample & request** tab.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- *If you accepted the wrong sample record before drying started →* Open the sample in **Samples**, click the **More actions** menu at the top right, and click **Undo intake**. Once **Air Drying** has been confirmed, **Undo intake** is no longer available.
- *If a sample must wait at reception while the client clarifies the order →* Open the sample in **Samples** and check **Sample holds**. While a sample has an **Open** hold (**Sample held**), bench work is paused until a manager clicks **Resolve hold**, enters **Resolution reason (required)**, and saves.

---

## For the QA Person

- Physical responsibility for the sample is tracked continuously from the receiving officer (**Receiving Officer** and **Custody Carrier** (person who brought the samples) at intake) through every shelf transfer logged via **Record storage movement**.
- Undoing an intake (**Undo intake**) is restricted to samples that have not started preparation or analysis, and is logged in the sample's permanent audit history.
