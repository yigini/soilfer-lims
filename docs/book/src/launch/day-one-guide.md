# Day-One & Week-One Parallel-Run Guide

> *Time:* 10 minutes each morning and afternoon during Week 1.
> *Who can do this:* Laboratory Manager, QA Officer, and all bench and reception staff.

During your first week with SoilFER LIMS, keep your existing paper logbook or Excel sheets on Monday morning. Run a calm five-day parallel run on one or two real sample boxes. Your team will build confidence while your existing workflow stays safe.

---

## Golden Rules for Week 1

1. *Pick a manageable first batch:* Start on Day 1 with one real box of 10–20 routine soil samples. Choose 2–3 familiar tests such as Soil pH, Electrical Conductivity, and Organic Carbon.
2. *Keep your paper or Excel log alongside the LIMS for five days:* Enter intake, drying, and bench readings into SoilFER LIMS at the bench. Compare the final **Certificate of Analysis** PDF against your usual sheet before releasing it to the client.
3. *Never share logins:* Even when two technicians share one bench computer, each person signs in with their own account. Staff can also use their personal bench PIN when **Require PIN when recording** is enabled.
4. *Write down questions instead of getting stuck:* Check the [Printable Bench Cards & Posters](../print/index.md) or **Help** drawer first. If a screen still blocks you after two minutes, jot the sample code and screen name on the [One-Page Pilot Feedback Form](./pilot-feedback-form.md) and ask the Laboratory Manager at the afternoon check-in.

---

## Day-by-Day Schedule for Week 1

| Day | Focus in the Laboratory | 10-Minute Afternoon Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Day 1 (Monday)* | Receive your first real box in **Sample Reception**, print **Standard (101 × 54 mm)** barcode labels, stick them on the bags, and start drying in **Workbench**. | Did every bag get a readable barcode label? Did the scanner read them cleanly? |
| *Day 2 (Tuesday)* | Complete drying (**Confirm Complete**) and 2 mm sieving records. Assign bench tests to technicians in **Manager Task List**. | Did all samples move from **Accepted** to **Processing** without stuck preparation gates? |
| *Day 3 (Wednesday)* | Run your first wet-chemistry batches in **Workbench** with a **Blank**, **Duplicate**, and **Control** (**Laboratory reference material (LRM)** or **Certified reference material (CRM)**). Try pasting one run from Excel. | Did the decimal separator and batch QC slots match your bench worksheet? |
| *Day 4 (Thursday)* | Review submitted batches in **Manager Task List**, inspect control charts in **QA Dashboard**, select reported values, and click **Approve**. | Did any batch trigger an unexpected QC warning or failure? Were the policy limits in **Policies** appropriate for your methods? |
| *Day 5 (Friday)* | Issue your first **Certificate of Analysis** PDF in **Reports**, compare it line by line against your parallel sheet, and collect everyone's [One-Page Pilot Feedback Form](./pilot-feedback-form.md). | Do the numbers, units, and branding on the PDF match your parallel sheet 100%? |

## How to Get Help During the Pilot

1. *On the bench:* Check the A4 card pinned next to your station ([Printable Bench Cards & Posters](../print/index.md)). You can also click the **Help** button on your screen to open the step-by-step guide for that page.
2. *Inside your laboratory:* Ask your **Laboratory Manager** or **Quality & Audit Officer** if a sample is **On Hold**. Also ask them when a batch QC check fails or an instrument shows **Calibration Overdue / Blocked**.
3. *Pilot support contact:* If you find a system error, email the support contact shown at the bottom of the **Sign in to SoilFER LIMS** screen. Include the sample code, the page name, and the exact message on screen. See [Getting Help](../start/getting-help.md).

### For the QA Person

- *Parallel-run reconciliation:* Compare the LIMS **Certificate of Analysis** PDF against your legacy calculation sheet on Day 5. This comparison provides documented method verification before you retire manual spreadsheets.
