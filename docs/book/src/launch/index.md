# Pilot Launch Kit

## What This Is For

Everything a laboratory manager and trainer need to introduce SoilFER LIMS to staff, run hands-on training sessions, and manage the first week of live testing.

---

## What Is in the Launch Kit

1. **Go-Live Checklist:** Step-by-step setup list for the laboratory administrator before day one. See [Lab Go-Live Checklist](../admin/go-live-checklist.md).
2. **Role First-Day Guides & Daily Workflows:**
   - Reception: [Sample Reception & Intake](../usage/reception-and-intake.md)
   - Preparation: [Drying, Preparation, Storage & Custody](../usage/preparation-and-custody.md)
   - Bench Technicians: [Technician Workbench & Analytical Runs](../usage/technician-workbench.md)
   - Managers & QA: [Quality Control, Control Charts & Failed QC](../usage/qc-management.md) and [Review, Approval & Plausibility Checks](../usage/review-and-approval.md)
3. **Printable Bench Cards:** One-page wall cards for reception, the wet-lab bench, and the manager's desk. See [Printable Bench Cards & Posters](../print/index.md).
4. **Getting Help & Reporting Pilot Feedback:** How to use the in-app **Help** button, work offline, and send clear feedback during the pilot. See [Getting Help](../start/getting-help.md).
