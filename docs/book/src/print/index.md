# Printable Bench Cards & Posters

## What This Is For

One-page reference cards designed to be printed on A4 paper and pinned next to the reception desk, wet-lab benches, and the laboratory manager's computer.

---

## Available Print Cards

- **Quick Reference Card:** Essential daily steps, roles, and shortcuts on one page. See [Quick Reference Card](../reference/quick-reference.md).
- **Status Reference:** Plain-language guide to sample, batch, and report statuses. See [Statuses A to Z](../reference/statuses-a-to-z.md).
- **Reception, Bench, Manager, Sample Journey & QC Decision Cards:** Printable wall cards for each station in the laboratory.

To print any page in this manual, click the printer icon in the top bar of the book reader and choose **Save as PDF** or your laboratory printer.
