# Printable Bench Cards & Posters

> *Time:* 5 minutes to print on A4 paper.
> *Who can do this:* Laboratory Managers, Administrators, and trainers preparing the laboratory before day one.

These one-page A4 reference cards and wall posters are designed to be printed and pinned right next to the reception counter, the sample preparation room, the wet-chemistry benches, and the laboratory manager's desk.

## How to Print Cleanly on A4 Paper

1. Open the card you want to print from the table above.
2. Click the printer icon in the top-right bar of this manual (or press Ctrl+P on Windows / Cmd+P on Mac).
3. Select your printer (or save as a PDF), choose *A4* paper size, and set margins to *Default* or *Minimum* so the entire card fits on one single sheet.
4. Put the printed sheet inside a clear plastic sleeve if it will sit near wet-chemistry benches or soil sieves.
