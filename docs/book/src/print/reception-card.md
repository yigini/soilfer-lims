# Reception Card: Receiving a Box in 8 Steps

> *Pin this A4 card next to the reception desk computer and label printer.*

---

## Receiving Today's Box of Samples

1. *Sign in with your own account:* On **Sign in to SoilFER LIMS**, enter your **Username** and **Password** and click **Sign In**.
2. *Open Sample Reception:* Click **Sample Reception** in the left menu (or click **Walk-in Intake** for a single client bringing bags without a pre-registered manifest).
3. *Find the box or expected samples:* Look under **Expected Arrivals** for samples sent from **Projects** or **KoboToolbox Integration** (**Expected**), or upload the shipment manifest spreadsheet.
4. *Check the physical bag count and condition:* Count the soil bags in the box. Inspect each bag to confirm the label is readable, the bag is dry and intact, and there is enough soil for testing.
5. *Accept healthy bags:* Select each intact bag, verify its field ID, depth, and test package, and click **Accept Sample**. Its status changes to **Accepted** and SoilFER LIMS issues a permanent laboratory sample code.
6. *Flag damaged or problem bags immediately (never guess):*
   - *Torn, leaking, or not enough soil:* Mark the bag **Rejected (problem recorded)** and record the reason.
   - *Missing paperwork or unclear field ID:* Place the sample **On Hold** with a short note and tell your **Laboratory Manager**.
   - *Need to step away mid-box:* Click **Save Draft** (**Draft saved**) and resume later.
7. *Print and stick on barcode labels:* Click **Print labels**. Choose **Standard (101 × 54 mm)** for main sample bags or **Compact (50 × 25 mm)** for small containers. Set **Copies per label**, and stick each label firmly onto its matching bag.
8. *Hand over to preparation:* Place the labelled bags in the drying tray. Log the sample handoff (**Moved** or **Issued**) so technicians see them in **My Work** and **Workbench**.

## Quick Troubleshooting at the Counter

| What You See on Screen | What You Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sample ID {id} was already checked in** | That field sample ID was already received earlier. Check whether this bag is a second depth layer from the same pit or a duplicate shipment before creating a new entry. |
| *Sample stays in* **Expected** *and will not print a permanent label* | An **Expected** row is only a field pre-registration. Physically inspect the bag and click **Accept Sample** first. |
| *Label barcode will not scan* | Check that the printer ribbon or thermal head is clean, reprint using **Print labels**, or type the laboratory sample code into the search box. |
