# Finding Your Way Around the Screen

## What This Is For

Learn what each menu item and top-bar button does after you sign in to SoilFER LIMS.

---

## Before You Start

- Sign in with your personal username and password on the **Sign in to SoilFER LIMS** screen.
- If this is your first sign-in with a temporary password, complete the **Security Update Required** window and click **Update & Access System**.

---

## 1. The Left Navigation Menu

The menu on the left side of the screen shows only the pages your role uses. You can click **Collapse sidebar** at the bottom of the menu to make more room for wide tables, or **Expand sidebar** to open it again.

| Menu Name | Who Sees It | What You Do There |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dashboard** | Everyone | See today's laboratory summary and quick shortcuts. |
| **Projects** | Everyone | View sampling projects, field forms, and project sample lists. |
| **Sample Reception** | Reception, Lab Manager | Check in arriving soil samples, record condition, and print labels. |
| **Samples** | Everyone | Search and track any sample in your laboratory. |
| **My Work** | Lab Technician | See the preparation and analytical tasks assigned to you today. |
| **Workbench** | Lab Technician | Build analytical batches, enter or paste readings, and send runs for review. |
| **Manager Task List** | Lab Manager | Assign work, review completed runs, and handle quality control alerts. |
| **Quality Assurance** | Lab Manager, Auditor | Inspect control charts, quality alerts, proficiency tests, and nonconformities. |
| **Data Results** | Lab Manager, Project Manager | Browse and export reviewed analytical results across projects. |
| **Result Reports** | Everyone | View, approve, and download PDF Certificates of Analysis. |
| **Spectral Library** | Technician, Manager, Viewer | Upload and inspect mid-infrared and near-infrared soil spectra. |
| **Inventory** | Technician, Lab Manager | Check reagent stock levels and lot expiry dates. |
| **Equipment** | Reception, Technician, Manager, Auditor | Check instrument status, calibration dates, and reference materials. |
| **Laboratory Staff** | Lab Manager | Create staff accounts, assign roles, and reset forgotten passwords. |
| **My Laboratory** / **Lab Management** | Lab Manager, Administrator | Update your laboratory name, address, logo, and active methods. |
| **Admin Panel** | Lab Manager, Administrator | Manage branding, translations, analysis catalogue, and audit logs. |
| **Policies** | Everyone | View or update your laboratory's quality rules and batch settings. |
| **About SoilFER** | Everyone | Read information about the SoilFER programme and software release. |

---

## 2. The Top Bar

The bar across the top of the screen stays visible on every page. From left to right on the right side, you will find:

1. **Notifications** (bell icon): Opens **Messages & Alerts** so you can read laboratory notifications or send a message to a colleague.
2. **Help**: Opens the **Help with this page** drawer with step-by-step instructions for your current screen.
3. **Language switcher**: Switches the screen and help text between English, Spanish, Latin American Spanish, French, and Portuguese.
4. **Theme toggle**: Switches the screen between light mode and dark mode.
5. **User Menu** (your name and role): Shows your active role and time zone. Click it to open **Profile Settings** (where you change your password or bench PIN) or click **Sign Out** when your shift ends.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **If you do not see a menu item you need →** Check your role under your name in the **User Menu** at the top right. Ask your Laboratory Manager to check your role in **Laboratory Staff**.
- **If the screen is in the wrong language →** Click the language button in the top bar and pick your preferred language.
- **If you share a computer with other technicians →** Always click your name in the top right and click **Sign Out** before leaving the bench, or use your personal bench PIN when recording results.
