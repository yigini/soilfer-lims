# Getting Help

## What This Is For

Find quick answers while you work, even if your laboratory internet connection is temporarily down, and know what to send if you need support.

---

## Before You Start

- You are signed in to SoilFER LIMS on any screen.

---

## Steps

### 1. Open Help for the Screen in Front of You

1. Look at the top bar and click **Help**.
   *You should see the **Help with this page** panel slide open on the right side of the screen.*
2. Read the short summary and numbered steps while keeping your work open on the left.
3. If you want the full article with field tables and troubleshooting tips, click **Read the guide**.
4. When you finish, click **Close help** to return to your work.

### 2. Search All Help Topics and Common Questions

1. Open the **Help Centre** from the help drawer or navigate to the help page.
   *You should see **How can we help at the lab?** with topic cards and a search box.*
2. Type a short word into the search box (such as *blank*, *label*, *password*, or *pH*), or click **Common Questions (FAQs)** and use **Filter questions...** to narrow the list.
3. Click any article to read it, or click **Print** inside an article to print a paper copy for your bench.

### 3. Open the Full Manual

Click **Manual** at the bottom of the Help drawer to open the full user manual in a new browser tab. The link is shown when your installation has a Manual URL configured. Keep the in-app Help drawer for guidance beside the screen where you are working.

### 4. Use Help When the Internet Is Offline

1. Once you open SoilFER LIMS in your browser, the help guides are saved on your computer for offline reading.
2. If your internet connection drops, you will see **Downloaded Help (Offline)** or **Offline mode: reading locally synchronized copy.** at the top of the help panel.
   *You can still read every downloaded guide until your connection returns.*

---

## What Happens Next

At the bottom of any help article, you can click **Yes** or **No** next to **Was this guidance helpful?** to tell the team if a step was clear or confusing. Your feedback never sends personal data or sample results.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **If you forget your password →** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and ask your Laboratory Manager to issue a temporary password in **Laboratory Staff**.
- **If a sample or batch is blocked and you do not know why →** Click **Help** in the top bar. The help panel automatically highlights guidance for any active warning on your screen.
- **If you need to report a problem to your Laboratory Manager or pilot support →** Write down four things before you call or message:
  1. Which screen you were on (for example, **Sample Reception** or **Workbench**).
  2. The sample code or batch number you were working on.
  3. The exact message shown on screen (or take a screenshot that does not show passwords).
  4. Approximately what time it happened.
