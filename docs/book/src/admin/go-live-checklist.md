# Lab Go-Live Checklist

## What This Is For

Use this checklist to prepare your soil laboratory in SoilFER LIMS before your team receives its first real batch of samples.

---

## Before You Start

- Sign in with a **Laboratory Manager** or administrator account.

---

## Checklist Overview

Tick off each step in order, following the linked guide for details:

- [ ] **1. First sign-in and administrator password:** Sign in for the first time and set a personal password of at least 12 characters. See [First Login & Lab Setup](../usage/first-login.md).
- [ ] **2. Laboratory identity and report branding:** Enter your laboratory name, code, address, time zone, and logo in **My Laboratory** and **Admin Panel**.
- [ ] **3. Staff accounts and roles:** Create an account for each reception clerk, technician, reviewer, and manager in **Laboratory Staff**, and have each person sign in once to set their own password. See [Roles, Permissions & Separation of Duties](../usage/roles.md).
- [ ] **4. Quality preset and laboratory rules:** Choose your laboratory's quality preset and check your batch rules in **Policies**. See [Per-Lab Settings, Presets & Method Overrides](../usage/lab-settings-and-presets.md).
- [ ] **5. Analytical methods and quality limits:** Switch on the soil tests your laboratory performs and check the blank, duplicate, and reference soil limits for each method. See [Quality Control, Control Charts & Failed QC](../usage/qc-management.md).
- [ ] **6. Equipment and reference materials:** Register your balances, pH meters, spectrophotometers, and drying ovens with their calibration due dates, and add your active reference soil lots in **Equipment**. See [Equipment Readiness, Reference Materials & PT](../usage/equipment-inventory.md).
- [ ] **7. Label printer and test report check:** Receive one practice sample in **Sample Reception**, print a test barcode label, run a practice batch in **Workbench**, and issue a practice PDF Certificate of Analysis in **Reports**.
- [ ] **8. Backups and support contact:** Confirm with your server administrator that daily backups are running and that your support contact is set. See [Backing Up Your Data](../maintenance/backups.md) and [Getting Help](../start/getting-help.md).
